A tornado is a violently rotating column of air extending from cloud to ground. A hurricane is a large rotating storm with strong winds over warm ocean water. A typhoon is the same as hurricane but occurs in Pacific Ocean. A cyclone is a rotating storm system with low pressure at center. Thunder is the loud sound caused by lightning heating air rapidly. Lightning is a bright flash of electricity between clouds or cloud and ground. A thunderstorm is a storm with thunder, lightning, heavy rain, and sometimes hail. A drought is a long period with little or no rainfall causing water shortage. A flood is when water overflows onto normally dry land from too much rain. An avalanche is a large mass of snow sliding rapidly down a mountain. A landslide is when soil and rocks slide down a slope after heavy rain. An earthquake is shaking of ground caused by movement of Earth's tectonic plates. A volcano is a mountain with opening where hot lava erupts from underground. Lava is hot molten rock that flows from volcano during eruption. Magma is molten rock beneath Earth's surface before it erupts. Ash is fine particles of rock blown into air during volcanic eruption. A crater is a bowl-shaped depression at top of volcano or from impact. A geyser is a hot spring that periodically erupts with steam and water. A hot spring is a natural pool of water heated by underground volcanic activity. A cave is a natural underground hollow space in rock or earth. A stalactite is an icicle-shaped formation hanging from cave ceiling made from minerals. A stalagmite is a cone-shaped formation rising from cave floor made from dripping water. A fossil is preserved remains of ancient plants or animals in rock. A dinosaur was a large reptile that lived millions of years ago and is now extinct. A mammoth was a large hairy elephant that lived during ice age. A saber-toothed tiger was a prehistoric cat with long sharp teeth. Evolution is the process by which species change over long periods of time. Extinction is when a species dies out completely and no longer exists. A habitat is the natural environment where a plant or animal lives. An ecosystem is a community of living things interacting with their environment. A food chain shows how energy passes from one organism to another through eating. A producer is an organism like plant that makes its own food from sunlight. A consumer is an organism that eats other organisms for energy. A predator is an animal that hunts and eats other animals for food. Prey is an animal that is hunted and eaten by predators. A carnivore is an animal that eats only meat from other animals. A herbivore is an animal that eats only plants and vegetation. An omnivore is an animal that eats both plants and animals. A scavenger is an animal that eats dead animals it finds rather than hunting. A decomposer is an organism that breaks down dead material into nutrients. Bacteria are tiny single-celled organisms found everywhere including inside bodies. A virus is a microscopic infectious agent that can cause diseases. A fungus is an organism like mushroom or mold that absorbs nutrients from surroundings. A mushroom is a fungus with stem and cap that grows in damp areas. Mold is a fuzzy fungus that grows on old food and damp surfaces. Yeast is a single-celled fungus used to make bread rise and ferment beverages. Algae are simple plant-like organisms that grow in water and make oxygen. Plankton are tiny organisms floating in oceans that are food for larger animals. Coral is a tiny marine animal that builds reefs in warm shallow oceans. A coral reef is an underwater structure made from skeletons of coral polyps. A sponge is a simple marine animal with porous body that filters water. A jellyfish is a soft transparent sea creature with tentacles that can sting. A starfish is a marine animal with five arms arranged in star shape. A sea urchin is a spiny round marine animal that lives on ocean floor. An octopus is a marine animal with eight tentacles and ability to change color. A squid is a marine animal with ten tentacles and ability to squirt ink. A crab is a crustacean with hard shell, claws, and walks sideways. A lobster is a large marine crustacean with big claws considered a delicacy. A shrimp is a small marine crustacean with long body and many legs. A clam is a bivalve mollusk with two shells that lives buried in sand. An oyster is a bivalve mollusk that sometimes forms pearls inside its shell. A mussel is a bivalve mollusk with dark elongated shell that attaches to rocks. A scallop is a bivalve mollusk with fan-shaped shell that can swim by clapping shells. A snail moves slowly on single muscular foot and carries spiral shell. A slug is like a snail but without shell and leaves slimy trail. An earthworm is a segmented worm that burrows through soil aerating it. A leech is a worm that attaches to animals and sucks blood. A centipede is a long creature with many segments each having pair of legs. A millipede is similar to centipede but with two pairs of legs per segment. A dragonfly is an insect with four transparent wings that hovers over water. A damselfly is similar to dragonfly but smaller with wings folded when resting. A grasshopper is a jumping insect with long back legs and makes chirping sounds. A cricket is similar to grasshopper but darker and chirps by rubbing wings together. A praying mantis is a green insect with folded front legs that catches other insects. A stick insect looks like a twig for camouflage and moves very slowly. A cicada is an insect that lives underground for years then emerges making loud buzzing. A firefly is a beetle that produces light from its abdomen at night. A moth is like a butterfly but flies at night and has feathery antennae. A caterpillar is the larva stage of butterfly that crawls and eats leaves. A cocoon is a silk case caterpillar makes where it transforms into butterfly. A chrysalis is the hard case where caterpillar transforms into butterfly. Metamorphosis is the transformation process from larva to adult in insects. A beetle has hard wing covers and is the most common type of insect. A ladybug is helpful beetle that eats aphids that damage plants. A lightning bug is another name for firefly that glows at night. A mosquito is a small flying insect that bites and sucks blood causing itchy bumps. A fly is a common insect with two wings that lands on food and spreads germs. A housefly is the common fly found around homes and garbage. A fruit fly is a tiny fly attracted to overripe fruit and fermenting materials. A horsefly is a large biting fly that bothers livestock and people. A tick is a small parasitic arachnid that attaches to skin and sucks blood. A flea is a tiny jumping insect that lives on mammals and bites to drink blood. A termite is a small insect that eats wood and can damage wooden structures. A cockroach is a fast-moving insect with flat body that lives in dark damp places. A wasp is a stinging insect with narrow waist and can sting multiple times. A hornet is a large aggressive wasp with painful sting that defends its nest. A yellow jacket is a type of wasp with black and yellow stripes that is very aggressive. A bumblebee is a large fuzzy bee that pollinates flowers and makes small hives. A honeybee lives in large colonies in hives and produces honey for food. A queen bee is the largest bee in colony that lays all the eggs. A worker bee is a female bee that collects pollen and makes honey. A drone is a male bee whose only job is to mate with queen. A beehive is a structure where colony of bees live and store honey. Honeycomb is the wax structure with hexagonal cells where bees store honey and eggs. Pollen is fine powder from flowers carried by bees and wind to fertilize plants. Nectar is sweet liquid produced by flowers to attract pollinators like bees. Pollination is the transfer of pollen from one flower to another enabling reproduction. A hummingbird is a tiny bird that hovers at flowers sipping nectar with long beak. A woodpecker is a bird that pecks holes in trees with strong beak seeking insects. A robin is a common songbird with red-orange breast that eats worms. A blue jay is a bright blue bird with crest that makes loud calls. A cardinal is a bright red bird with crest that sings beautifully. A sparrow is a small brown bird commonly found in cities and yards. A crow is a large black bird that is very intelligent and makes cawing sounds. A raven is a large black bird similar to crow but bigger with deeper call. An owl is a nocturnal bird with large eyes that hunts rodents at night. An eagle is a large powerful bird of prey with sharp talons and excellent vision. A hawk is a bird of prey that hunts small animals with sharp claws and beak. A falcon is a fast bird of prey used in falconry to hunt other birds. A vulture is a large bird that feeds on dead animals and has bald head. A seagull is a white and gray bird commonly found near oceans and coasts. A pelican is a large water bird with throat pouch for catching fish. A flamingo is a pink wading bird with long legs and curved beak. A swan is a large elegant water bird with long curved neck and white feathers. A goose is a large water bird that migrates in V-formation and honks loudly. A peacock is a bird with spectacular colorful tail feathers displayed in fan.
